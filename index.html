    How this works

    Only jobs in the United States (including US remote) are shown. Each company is matched to its public job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters or Workday) and a crawler reads those boards every day. Companies on other systems are listed with a link to their careers page. Seniority is worked out from the job title, so a few jobs may be in the wrong level. "Large" means about 1,000 or more employees. "First seen" is the first check that found the job, not the employer's posting date.

    The "not on LinkedIn or Indeed" filter uses a separate check that searches those sites for each job. It turns on once that check has run (see the README).
